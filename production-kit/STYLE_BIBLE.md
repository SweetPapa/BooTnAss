# BOOT N ASS (STARBURST) // Visual & Audio Style Bible

Use this guide alongside the reference images in `production-kit/references/` so every cutscene video and music track looks and sounds like it belongs in the exact same universe as the 3D game.

---

## 1. Visual Identity ("Star Fox 64 meets Rez Infinite meets 90s Saturday-Morning Arcade")
- **Lighting & Contrast**: Pitch-dark deep space / indigo void (`#040611` – `#07091a`) with crisp, high-contrast neon rim lighting. Never muddy or foggy gray.
- **Core Palette**:
  - **Hero / Player Ship (`VANGUARD-X`)**: Sleek angular white/titanium delta-wing starfighter with **Electric Cyan (`#00f0ff`)** twin plasma thrusters and **Gold (`#ffd447`)** overdrive trim.
  - **Hostiles / Bosses**: Dark charcoal/chrome armor with **Blazing Crimson / Hazard Orange (`#ff3818` / `#ff2a85`)** glowing cores and eyes.
  - **Sector 1 — Neon Canyon**: Deep indigo sky (`#07091a`), wireframe canyon walls (`#00f0ff`), giant sliced synthwave magenta sun (`#ff3d9a`), ringed cyan planet.
  - **Sector 2 — Prism Asteroid Belt**: Pitch-black nebula (`#040610`), glowing teal (`#00ffd5`) & violet (`#c84bff`) faceted crystal asteroids, colossal ringed gas giant.
  - **Sector 3 — Hyper-Grid Spire**: Obsidian monolith trench (`#060812`), pulsing gold (`#ffd447`) & electric cyan (`#00f0ff`) energy conduits, rotating Dyson megastructure rings.
- **Character Tone**: Bold, expressive 90s arcade cartoon comedy (think *Star Fox 64* + *Ratchet & Clank* + *Space Jam* / Saturday-morning action-comedy). Zero cringe/romance — pure high-energy action-comedy & trash talk!

## 3. Technical Specs for Drop-In Assets
- **Videos**: `16:9` aspect ratio (`1280x720` or `1920x1080`), `.mp4` (H.264 + AAC audio), `5–10 seconds` per cutscene (snappy arcade pacing, skippable with `Space` / `A`).
- **Music**: `.mp3` (192kbps+) or `.ogg`, seamlessly loopable for level & boss tracks (`60–120s` loops).
